I am a Master's student in Photonics at the [Abbe School of Photonics](https://www.asp.uni-jena.de/), University of Jena, Germany (expected graduation: Nov. 2026). My research interests lie in optical microscopy, computational imaging, and photonics.

I am currently working at the [Leibniz Institute of Photonic Technology (IPHT)](https://www.leibniz-ipht.de/), Microscopy Department, supervised by Prof. Dr. Rainer Heintzmann and Dr. Daniela Täuber, on live-cell imaging of F-Actin by polarization fluorescence microscopy.

Previously, I worked on Mid-IR spectroscopic imaging of retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) granules using PiF-IR, achieving ~5 nm spatial resolution and applying PCA for chemical heterogeneity analysis. I also worked on implementing MAGIC NOR logic gates using BiFeO3 memristors at the Quantum Detection Department of IPHT.

I hold a B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering from [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://www.hust.edu.cn/), where I worked on multimodal deep learning for Alzheimer's disease classification.
